Demolition Rules

• Every player starts with exactly 180 structural bricks.

• Darts thrown decrease your remaining score profile framework.

• You have exactly 3 darts per round before control passes.

• You must reach exactly zero. Going negative triggers a BUST event.

• If someone checks out, remaining players still finish the round.

• Multiple checkouts in a round go to a playoff starting at 60.
