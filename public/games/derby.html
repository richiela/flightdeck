Derby Rules

• Each player is assigned a target number on the board.

• Hit your own number to move forward (singles 1, doubles 2, triples 3).

• Hit another player’s number to knock them back the same amount.

• Finished horses can’t be knocked backward.

• First past the post opens the finish — remaining players finish the rotation.

• Match ends after 8 rounds, or when the finish rotation completes.
