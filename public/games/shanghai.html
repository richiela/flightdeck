Shanghai Rules

• Eight rounds — round 1 targets 1, round 2 targets 2, … through 8.

• Three darts each turn. Only hits on the current number score.

• Single = N, Double = 2×N, Triple = 3×N. Everything else is zero.

• Highest total after round 8 wins. Ties are a draw.
