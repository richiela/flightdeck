DEMOLITION

Demolition

Race the score to zero. Bust and you’re stuck — checkout and the tower falls.

Limbo

Clear under the bar to lower it — always three darts. Match holds; go over (or get stuck) and lose a life!

Derby

Hit your number to gallop ahead. Hit a rival’s and shove them back down the track.

Killer

Hit your mark to arm up. Hit theirs to take lives. Last player standing wins.

Quackshot

Duck hunt for bullseyes. Inner rings score, triples bite back — and the last round is worth double.

Shanghai

Eight rounds, one number each. Singles, doubles, and triples on the target count — highest total wins.

Warm Up

Shake out the arm. Three darts, one total — then stack it and throw again. No score to chase, just heat.

Quick 10

One player. Ten visits of three darts — chase the highest score and climb the leaderboard.

X01

Configurable 301–901. Dart-in / dart-out options. Bust ends the turn. Max 4 players.

Cricket

Close 20–15 and bull. Score while opponents still have them open. Max 4 players or teams.
