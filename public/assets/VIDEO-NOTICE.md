# A note about the video clips in here

The `.mp4` files under `shared/`, `demolition/`, and `killer/` are **example
clips only** — short excerpts from movies, TV shows, and a sports broadcast
(Karate Kid, a Super Bowl broadcast, SpongeBob, Rocky, Star Wars, Happy
Gilmore, Skyfall, and a couple others). I don't hold the rights to any of
them.

They're wired up purely to demonstrate that FlightDeck can play a short
video on a winner/bust/elimination screen — not as content meant to be kept,
reused, or redistributed. If you're running this for real, or forking it
into your own repo, **delete these and drop in your own clips first**. See
the top-level [README](../../README.md#videos) and
`public/games/event-videos.js` for how a game hooks a video up.
