Killer Rules

• Each player is assigned a unique target number.

• Hit your number to fill marks — doubles/triples count extra. Reach 3 marks to become a Killer.

• As a Killer, hit other players’ numbers to strip their marks/lives.

• Empty a civilian or drop a Killer below 3 marks to revoke their status.

• Overkill can eliminate a player outright.

• Rounds 1–6 are normal; 7–9 are ×2; 10–12 are ×3. Last player standing wins. If several survive round 12: sole Killer wins; multiple Killers share the win; if nobody is Killer, most wedges wins (ties share).
