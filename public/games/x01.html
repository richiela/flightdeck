X01 Rules

• Choose start score (301–901), dart-in, and dart-out on Control when the match begins. Max 4 players.

• Subtract each dart after you’re in. Three darts per visit.

• Finish must match dart-out (none / double / triple). Going below 0 busts the visit.

• Bust restores score to start of visit and ends the turn.
